# FREE.TV + FREE.RADIO

Free live TV and internet radio in your browser. No account, no install, no ads.

## Usage

Just open the HTML files in Chrome or Firefox. That's it.

- **`free_tv.html`** — live TV. Click a source button, pick a channel.
- **`free_radio.html`** — internet radio. Click a genre, pick a station.

`↑↓` to navigate · `/` to search · click to tune.

## Python Scripts (optional)

`build_tv.py` and `build_radio.py` fetch channels ahead of time and bake them into the HTML for offline use. Run either one with `python3 build_tv.py` or `python3 build_radio.py`.

## Sources

- TV streams from [iptv-org](https://github.com/iptv-org/iptv) and [Free-TV](https://github.com/Free-TV/IPTV)
- Radio stations from [radio-browser.info](https://www.radio-browser.info) (100,000+ stations)

Some TV streams are geo-restricted and may not play in all regions.
